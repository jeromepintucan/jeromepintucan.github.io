## 3. Worked explanations

### 3.1 Commission base and the double-count correction

Original venue formula (requirements §5): `venue_net = commissionable_base + non-commissionable venue charges − platform_commission − venue-funded discounts − venue-responsible adjustments − applicable refunds`, where the default `commissionable_base` is the booking base **after** discount. Synthetic example: base ₱400.00, venue-funded promo ₱40.00, commission 5%, no gateway fee passed through.

| Line | Original formula | Canonical formula |
|---|---|---|
| Booking base | ₱400.00 | ₱400.00 |
| Venue-funded discount | ₱40.00 | ₱40.00 |
| Commissionable base (after venue-funded discount) | ₱360.00 | ₱360.00 |
| Platform commission (5% = 50,000 ppm) | ₱18.00 | ₱18.00 |
| Customer pays | ₱360.00 | ₱360.00 |
| Venue net | 360.00 − 18.00 − 40.00 = **₱302.00** | 400.00 − 40.00 − 18.00 = **₱342.00** |
| Check: venue net + commission = amount collected? | ₱320.00 ≠ ₱360.00 (₱40.00 unallocated; ledger cannot balance) | ₱360.00 = ₱360.00 |

Canonical rule (brief §4): `venue_net = gross venue items − venue-funded discounts − platform_commission − venue-borne gateway fee − venue-responsible adjustments − venue share of refunds (− withholding tax, if applicable)` — the discount is subtracted exactly once. A **platform-funded** ₱40.00 promo leaves the commissionable base at ₱400.00: customer pays ₱360.00, commission ₱20.00, venue net ₱380.00, platform net −₱20.00 (₱40.00 to `platform:promotions_expense`); check 380.00 − 20.00 = 360.00.
